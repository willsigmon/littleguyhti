# HTI digital card

**Brand North Star:** A trustworthy, event-ready HTI contact card that makes a partnership conversation feel easy to start.

**Audience / personality brief:** Prospective donors, corporate partners, and community organizations meeting Will at an event (verified from the page’s booking and partnership calls to action). The intended feeling is **practical, credible, inviting** (inferred from HTI’s public brand guidance). The reference is an official nonprofit event leave-behind: direct contact details, a compact impact ledger, and one clear next action rather than a marketing landing page.

**Reference pull:** HTI’s Brand Board/Branding Guide supplies the orange, gold, charcoal, Geomanist-led visual language and calls for warm, technology-focused photography. A professionally printed event badge supplies the concise identity block and scannable footer; it is not a template for the layout.
